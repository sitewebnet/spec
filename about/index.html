Peter Karathe Githuru — also known as Kirathe — is an ICT and data analytics professional based in Njoro, Nakuru County, Kenya. The writer has a degree in Mathematics and Computer Science (Statistics Option) from the Technical University of Mombasa, and combines hands-on technical support with a sharp eye for data: Python, R, Stata, SQL, Excel, Tableau, and Power BI are his daily tools, alongside network administration, cybersecurity awareness, and IT infrastructure management. He is the writer behind SpecBytesKE, where he covers AI, cybersecurity, and practical technology for Kenya and beyond, and works in ICT product sales at SPEC COMPUTERS, helping clients match the right tech to the right problem.

The writer's experience spans both the field and the screen. He has led data teams at the Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC), where he delivered 100% accuracy ahead of schedule during a high-volume national data capture period; kept systems reliable and inventory precise as an ICT Technician and Inventory Assistant at Aquamist Ltd; and installed, configured, and maintained systems and peripherals as an ICT Technician at NET COMPUTERS. Earlier, as an ICT Attaché at Njoro Canning Ltd, he supported system upgrades, network maintenance, and early website development — the very threads that now shape his writing. Every role has reinforced the same principle: practical tech should solve real problems, not just look good on paper.

When he is not writing or fixing something, Peter is usually learning something new — most recently deepening his cybersecurity foundation through Cybrary and HP, and polishing his digital marketing skills through Google. He believes Africa's tech future will be built by people who roll up their sleeves, and he writes to make that future a little easier to understand.
